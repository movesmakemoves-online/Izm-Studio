About

Two Decades In Media. One Direct Line To The Person Doing The Work.

Digital Spectrum Labs grew out of over 20 years in the media business — starting in artist development, moving into filmmaking, and eventually building the websites and digital tools that artists, brands, and businesses actually needed. That range is the whole point: creative work and digital work depend on each other, and I've spent two decades on both sides of that line.

Everything on this site, I needed myself first — the website, the tools, the digital groundwork behind a real business. I started Digital Spectrum Labs to pass that same benefit on: sorting out your digital spectrum, the whole range of design, development, video, and AI a modern brand needs, handled in one place instead of by five different vendors who've never spoken to each other.

Who you actually get: me. Not an account manager, not a junior handed your project after the sales call. You deal directly with the person doing the work, which means nothing gets lost in translation and you always know exactly where things stand. For larger builds I bring in trusted freelance specialists I've worked with for years — but I stay the single point of contact, and I'm accountable for what gets delivered either way.

Every project is built at your pace, tailored to what you actually need — not a fixed template. Once it's built, it's yours: keep me on to manage and grow it, or take it and run it yourself. No lock-in, no pressure either way.

I work with small and growing businesses on full digital consultancy — and with bloggers, YouTubers, and musicians who need the same range of skills on a creator's budget. Past work spans music videos, short films, and the websites and brand systems behind them.
